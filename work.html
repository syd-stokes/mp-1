Work Experience

In spring of 2025, I worked as a Flight Software Developer at NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center (GSFC). While there, I developed GUI-based tool for NASA’s core Flight System (cFS), a flight software framework supporting missions such as Artemis II, Orion, and Gateway. More recently, I spent this past summer in Pasadena working at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) doing research on artificial intelligence for mineral mapping using thermal infrared remote sensing.
