Activities & Leadership

During my time at BU, I was an active member of the Girls Who Code chapter on campus. Girls Who Code is an international nonprofit organization dedicated to closing the gender gap in technology. Through this club at BU, we hosted various introductory workshops in Python, Java, GitHub, and web development (HTML, CSS). I also later joined the e-board and helped teach weekly courses on Python and basic programming principles to young girls in Boston middle and high schools by creating a well-rounded curriculum with relevant practice sets.
